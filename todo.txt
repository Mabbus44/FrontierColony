Game design:
- You are sent on a mission to colonize a distant planet in the hunt for resource. Due to a asteroid hit your ship you lost all equipment except the cloner. Everyone died except you.
- Start game with 1 human and a cloner.
	- The cloner produce humans at a constant speed, costing food.
	- Human also require a small amount of food for upkeep.
	- Cloner can be upgraded with special resources for higher speed, but greater cost per human.
- World map with biomes (ice (Aquillo), lava (vulcanus), swamp (gleba), grass (nauvis), desert (nauvis), i dont think fulgora should be representet).
- Each city its own small surface
- Build resource collecting buildings in cities
	- Each building has 5 collectors (humnans), and any amount of transporters/military support.
	- All humans in one building move as a team to a point on the map, collect resources, and return with them.
- Some resources can also be gathered inside cities, for example:
	- Basic resources to startup
	- Specialized mines for lategame high scale production (maybe). Should probably be slower per human, but last longer or not run out at all (like oil).
- Caravans can be built to move things between cities, or clear biters, or explores, or found new cities. They contain transport humans and military.
- Each square on the worlmap has a biter amount, biter types, and biter evolution
	- Amount is decresed when you kill biters
	- Types is different per biome
	- Evolution is different per biome and increase over time and by pollution
- No technologies
	- instead you are limited by materials only existing in sertain biomes
	- and some things only being buildable in certain biomes.-
	- And you are limited where you can expand by your weponds and the biters in each biome.
	- There should be a specific order you expand in, probably grass -> desert -> swamp -> lava -> ice
- Goal of the game is building a spaceship capable of transporting resources to your homeworld
